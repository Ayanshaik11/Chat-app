2. Information the app may process

Depending on the features you use and how the app is configured, King X may process the following information:

King X is not intended to collect precise device location, and the developer has indicated that the app does not request location for its features. This statement should be rechecked against the app's permissions and all included SDKs before publication.

Do not send sensitive personal information in profiles, posts, or messages unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it with the intended recipients.
