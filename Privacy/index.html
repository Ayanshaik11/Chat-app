Privacy Policy

This page explains what information King X handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective: 5 November 2026 Developer: Developer King Ayan

2. Information the app may handle

  • Account and profile: Google sign-in identifiers and information such as your name, email address and profile photo, along with profile details you choose to add.
  • Chats and social content: messages, message metadata, friend requests, reactions, comments, posts, stories, reels and content you share.
  • Media: photos, videos and voice recordings you upload or send, together with media links and file details.
  • Device and notifications: push-notification token, platform, token update time and technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.
  • King X AI: prompts and conversation context you submit, AI responses and conversation records associated with your account.
  • Preferences: settings and app data used for themes, online status, notifications and other features.

Avoid submitting passwords, financial details or highly sensitive information to the app or its AI assistant.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, messaging, profiles, social features, media sharing, notifications, app updates, King X AI, support, security and service maintenance.

For example, notification tokens help deliver notifications, while prompts sent to King X AI are processed to generate responses.

4. Message encryption and media

Text messages: King X implements end-to-end encryption for text-message content and encrypted reply text when encryption is active and the recipient has a compatible encryption key. Private keys are stored on the user's device, and public keys are used to establish message encryption.

Important limitations: this encryption does not cover all chat information. Participant identifiers, timestamps, delivery/read status and other message metadata are not necessarily encrypted.

Photos, videos, voice messages and GIF content or links are not protected by the same end-to-end message encryption. Image captions and reply text may be encrypted, but the media itself is not.

Posts, stories, reels, profile photos and other uploaded media are not end-to-end encrypted by the chat-message system. Their visibility depends on the feature, provider configuration and app access rules.

Encryption helps protect message content, but it does not guarantee that every part of King X or every type of content is private.

5. Storage, visibility and service providers

  • Google Firebase: authentication and app data such as profiles, chats, social content, notification tokens and AI conversation records.
  • Cloudinary: upload and delivery of images, videos and audio files.
  • King X AI providers: AI requests may be processed by configured providers including Cloudflare, Groq, Google Gemini and OpenRouter. The active provider can vary. Prompts and relevant conversation context may be sent to the provider.
  • YouTube / Google: YouTube-related video discovery, playback and embedded content.
  • GIPHY: GIF search and GIF content delivery.
  • Apple iTunes and music services: music search or playback features.
  • Hosting providers: services such as Vercel and GitHub may host web pages, the AI backend or release downloads.

These providers have their own terms and privacy policies. King X does not control their independent privacy practices.

Some profiles and social content may be visible to other signed-in users depending on the feature and its access rules. Do not assume every profile field, post, story, reel or uploaded media item is visible only to friends.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, King X can register a device push token and store it with your user record to deliver notifications. Message notifications are designed to use generic message text rather than the contents of encrypted text messages. Platform push-notification services handle delivery.

You can control notification permissions in your device settings.

7. Retention and deletion requests

Information is retained as needed to operate the app, maintain conversations and social features, address security issues and meet operational requirements. Third-party providers may have their own retention practices.

The current app settings provide a log-out option but do not provide a complete in-app account-deletion control. To request deletion of your account and associated information, contact the developer by email. Your request may require account verification. Some information may remain temporarily for legal, security or operational reasons or in provider backups.

8. Security

King X uses measures including Firebase authentication and access rules, encrypted text-message content where supported, and device secure storage for message private keys.

No internet-connected service, storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Security also depends on correct configuration, device security, account access and third-party providers.

9. Children's privacy

King X is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Your choices

  • Choose what profile information and content you share.
  • Control camera, photo-library and notification permissions through device settings.
  • Choose whether to use optional features such as King X AI, media sharing and YouTube content.
  • Request account or personal-data deletion by contacting the developer.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when King X features, providers or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
